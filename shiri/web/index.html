    Recorded timing evidence

    Speaker calibration

    Record one speaker on each channel of a stereo capture device. Both microphones must share one ADC clock. A single microphone hearing both speakers, or separate phone recordings, cannot identify their delays.

    Keep positions, gain and capture processing fixed. Document network load and playback restarts in your exported evidence.

    For different zones, play the same probe through both using the phone's native grouping controls. Describe that group here. Shiri records your declaration; it cannot certify the phone group from imported audio.

    Measurement bounds

    Use zero for matched distances. This declared geometry correction is subtracted from the measured lag; it is not inferred from the recording.

    1. Download quiet test probe

      Play this WAV through the measured room or native grouped zones from one music source. Stop normal music first. The test has eight distinct bursts; do not mix in spoken replies.

    2. Capture both speakers simultaneously. Use uncompressed 16-bit stereo WAV, 16–96 kHz, no larger than 2 MiB. Keep the first and last burst complete. Make at least three fresh takes across playback restarts.

    3. Import each take below. Raw audio is discarded after analysis. Measurement summaries survive restarts: unfinished sessions last one hour; saved correction receipts last up to 90 days, within a 128-session history limit. Export evidence you need to keep.
